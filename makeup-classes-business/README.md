## The five things to do first

1. **Call your insurer** and confirm in writing that your policy covers *teaching and instruction to members of the public, including minors* — not just artistry on clients. This is the most common uninsured gap in this business.
2. **Apply for your Working With Children Check.** Processing takes time; start it before anything else.
3. **Audit your foundation shade range.** You need 12+ shades across warm, cool and neutral undertones before class one. Not being able to match a student is the worst thing that can happen in your room.
4. **Do a timed practice run** of the full 3-hour plan on two friends, for free. You will find it runs long, and you'll find out exactly where.
5. **Book the venue for three dates**, not one. Momentum sells the second and third class; a single date has nowhere to send people.
