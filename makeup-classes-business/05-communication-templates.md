> **How's the practice going?** If anything hasn't worked the way it did in the room, tell me what's happening and I'll troubleshoot it by reply. Genuinely — that's included, not an extra. The three most common week-one messages I get are:
> - *"It looked great and then went patchy by lunchtime"* → almost always a prep or powder-placement issue
> - *"My eyeshadow looks muddy"* → usually too much product on the brush at the transition stage
> - *"I can't match what you did on my brows"* → brush up first, then fill only the gaps. Never outline.
>
> Just reply. I read everything.
