## 3.1 Pricing

Prices below are in AUD and pitched at a metropolitan/regional Australian market for a skilled artist with a strong portfolio but a new teaching business. Adjust the whole ladder up or down together — never discount one product in isolation, because it breaks the logic of the others.

### The core ladder

| Product | Length | Group size | Price (per person) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Group Masterclass** | 3 hrs | 6–8 | **$145** | Your flagship. Launch price $115 for the first 3 classes, advertised explicitly as a *founding price for the first three classes* so it doesn't become the anchor. |
| **Teen Class (Fresh Faces)** | 3 hrs | 6–8 | **$125** | Slightly lower — parents are price-sensitive and this is a volume product. |
| **Duo Session** | 2.5 hrs | 2 | **$220 total** ($110 each) | Friends, mother/daughter, bride + bridesmaid. Sells surprisingly well. |
| **Mother & Daughter Masterclass** | 3 hrs | 4 pairs | **$230 per pair** | Group format, sold as a pair. Highest emotional value, best referrals. |
| **Private One-to-One** | 2 hrs | 1 | **$260** | Tailored to their face and their kit. Your highest hourly rate — this is the upsell you steer people toward. |
| **Private + Kit Audit** | 2.5 hrs | 1 | **$320** | They bring their whole makeup bag, you sort it: keep, bin, replace. Extraordinarily popular and costs you nothing but time. |
| **Birthday Party — Teen** | 2 hrs | 6–10 | **$65/head, min $450** | Simplified curriculum: prep, base, soft eye, lip, photos. No evening look. |
| **Birthday Party — Adult / Hens** | 2.5 hrs | 6–12 | **$85/head, min $600** | Add the evening look and a glass of something if the venue permits. |
| **Corporate / Team Workshop** | 2 hrs | 8–15 | **$1,200 flat** | "Camera-ready in 10 minutes" — for professional services firms. Price on value, not headcount. |
| **Class Two: Wings, Lashes & Colour** | 3 hrs | 6–8 | **$155** | Your retention product. Only sell it to people who have done Class One. |

### Add-ons and structural pricing

| Item | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Starter Kit (add-on at booking) | **$75** | Brush set, sponge, palette tile, headband, mirror, disposables, branded bag. Costs you ~$35. |
| Gift voucher | Face value | Zero-cost cash flow. Push hard in Nov–Dec and around Mother's Day. |
| Returning-student rate | **15% off** any future class | Say it at the close of every class. |
| Bring-a-friend | **$20 off each** | Your single most effective acquisition tool. Cheaper than any ad. |
| Deposit to hold a seat | **$50**, non-refundable inside 7 days | Balance due 48 hrs before. Kills no-shows. |

### Pricing principles

- **Never run an unadvertised discount.** Every reduction needs a stated reason (founding price, bring-a-friend, returning student, seasonal voucher) or you train people to wait for a sale.
- **Raise the price after the third class**, not the tenth. Founding-price classes exist to generate photographs and testimonials, and once you have both, the discount has done its job.
- **Sell the private session from inside the group class.** Roughly one in six group students will convert if you mention it once, warmly, at the close.
- **Do not compete on price with beauty schools.** You are selling three hours of a working artist's attention in a room of eight, not a certificate. Say that.

### What a full class actually earns

8 students × $145 = **$1,160 revenue**
Less: venue ~$120 · consumables and disposables ~$90 · printing ~$25 · refreshments ~$40 · card fees ~$25 = **~$300 costs**
**Net per class ≈ $860** for roughly 6 hours all-in (prep, delivery, pack-down).

## 3.4 Booking, payment and parent communication systems

Keep the whole stack under $60/month. Do not build a custom website before class ten.

### Recommended stack

| Function | Tool | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking + calendar | **Square Appointments** or **Fresha** | Free–$30/mo | Takes deposits, sends automatic reminders, has a public booking page you can link from Instagram. |
| Payment | **Square** or **Stripe** | ~1.7–2.2% per transaction | Card, Apple Pay, and payment links you can send by SMS. |
| Forms | **Jotform** or **Google Forms** | Free | Consent form as a mandatory step in the booking flow — no form, no seat. |
| Email | **MailerLite** | Free to 1,000 subscribers | Confirmations, follow-up sequence, newsletter. |
| SMS | Built into Square/Fresha, or **ClickSend** | Cents per message | Reminders. SMS open rates beat email by a distance. |
| Files and photos | **Google Drive**, password-protected folder | Free | Consent forms, release status, class photos. |
| Accounting | **Wave** (free) or **Xero** | $0–$35/mo | Start free; move to Xero when you register for GST. |

### The booking flow (build it exactly like this)

1. Student clicks the link in your Instagram bio → booking page.
2. Chooses class, date, and optional $75 Starter Kit.
3. **Consent + medical form is a required step** before payment can complete. For under-18s, the form asks for a parent's name, email and mobile — and the confirmation is sent to the *parent's* address, not the student's.
4. Pays a $50 deposit (or in full).
5. Automatic confirmation email + SMS (templates in section 5).
6. T-minus 48 hrs: balance charged automatically.
7. T-minus 24 hrs: reminder SMS.
8. T-plus 4 hrs: thank-you SMS.
9. T-plus 3 days: notes and photos email.
10. T-plus 12 days: rebooking offer.

### Parent communication rules

- **Every communication about a minor goes to the parent's email and mobile.** The student may be copied; the parent is never left off.
- The confirmation email to a parent must contain, without them having to ask: your full name and business name, your insurance status, your Working With Children Check number, the exact venue address, the exact start and finish times, whether they are welcome to stay (they are), your mobile number for the duration of the class, and the photo policy.
- **Offer parents the choice to stay explicitly.** *"You are very welcome to stay for the session — several parents do, and there's tea. You're equally welcome to drop off and collect at 1pm. Whatever suits you."* This one sentence converts more hesitant parents than anything else you can write.
- Send a short post-class note to parents of minors: what was covered, how their child went, and a genuine positive observation. Takes two minutes and generates a disproportionate amount of word-of-mouth.
- Never message a minor on a personal channel, and never post their face without the release ticked.

### Cancellation policy (publish it, then hold it kindly)

> Deposits are non-refundable within 7 days of the class, as your seat is held and your products are prepared for you. More than 7 days out, your deposit transfers to any future class. If you are unwell, please don't come — message me and I will move you to the next available date at no cost. No-shows forfeit the deposit.

The "if you're unwell, I'll move you free" clause costs you almost nothing and buys enormous goodwill — and it stops sick people bringing infections into a room where everyone is touching their own face.

## 3.5 Practical use of the community space

### Before your first class — walk the room with this checklist
- [ ] How many power points, and where? (You need at least two, for lighting and music.)
- [ ] Is there a sink with hot water in the room, or only down a corridor? This determines whether you can do a live double-cleanse demo or need basins and jugs.
- [ ] What is the natural light like at your class time, specifically? Visit at exactly the hour you plan to teach. North-facing windows are ideal; fluorescent overheads are a problem.
- [ ] Tables and chairs — how many, what size, who sets them up, and is there a setup/pack-down time allowance in your booking?
- [ ] Bathroom access and cleanliness.
- [ ] Parking, and is it safe and lit if you ever run an evening class?
- [ ] Wi-Fi (for card payments — and always have a mobile hotspot as backup).
- [ ] Can you play music? Can you mount or lean a TV/monitor?
- [ ] Storage — can you leave a locked trolley on site between classes? This saves you hours.
- [ ] Kitchen access for refreshments.
- [ ] Emergency exits, first aid kit location, and the venue's incident procedure.
- [ ] Is there another group in the building at the same time (noise, shared bathrooms, foot traffic past your door)?

### Room layout that works

**Preferred: a single long table, students on one side, you on the other, facing them.** Everyone sees you, you see everyone, and you can walk the length of the table in seconds. Each station gets ~80cm of table width.

Failing that, a shallow U-shape with you at the open end. Avoid scattered small tables — you lose the room.

**Lighting is the thing that will most affect your reviews.** Community halls are typically lit with cool overhead fluorescents that make everybody look ill and make colour-matching genuinely inaccurate. Fix it:
- Position tables to catch window light on the students' faces (facing the window, not backlit).
- Bring **two 5500K daylight LED panels or ring lights on stands**, placed in front of and slightly above the students. Budget $150–$250 for the pair; it is the highest-return equipment purchase you will make.
- If the overheads are badly coloured, turn them off entirely and light the room yourself.
- Have one station near the window designated the "true light check" spot, and walk each student to it for their colour match. This doubles as a nice teaching moment.

### Your standard kit-out (a trolley you can wheel in)

Daylight lights ×2 · desk mirrors ×10 · one large mirror for demos · phone tripod + HDMI/casting lead · extension lead + power board · Bluetooth speaker · tablecloths (plain white or oat — they photograph well and hide a scuffed community-hall table) · your product trolley · disposables caddy · lidded bin + liners · first aid kit · printed forms, notes, feedback cards, business cards · card reader · water jug and cups · a small vase of flowers and a folded sign at the door

### Timing your booking

Book the room for **4 hours** for a 3-hour class: 45 minutes setup, 3 hours delivery, 15 minutes pack-down. Never book back-to-back with no gap — you will run over on the first one.

**Best slots:** Saturday 10am–1pm (adults, best natural light) · Sunday 1pm–4pm (mother/daughter) · school holiday weekday 10am–1pm (teens — these sell out fastest) · Thursday 6–9pm (working adults, but you must bring your own lighting).

### Making a community hall look like a brand

Three cheap moves that transform the photographs, which is what actually matters for marketing:
1. **Plain tablecloths** in oat, white or your brand colour. Instantly removes "school hall".
2. **A clean corner** — one wall or window area kept clear, with good light, used as your photo backdrop for every finished face. Consistent backgrounds make your Instagram grid look professional.
3. **A branded A-frame or foam-board sign** at the door and a small flower arrangement on the table. Total cost under $150, and it is in every photo you take for the next two years.
