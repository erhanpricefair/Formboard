About

Hello — I'm Aphrodite

I've spent fifteen years doing makeup professionally: runway shows, editorial shoots, and more brides than I can count.

And in all that time, the thing I've heard most often isn't a question about technique. It's a version of the same sentence: "I don't really know what I'm doing." From women in their forties. From teenagers. From people who own drawers full of products they've never understood.

That's why this class exists. Not to make you look like someone else — to teach you your own face, properly, once, so you stop guessing.

Questions

Everything people ask

I'm a total beginner. Will I be out of my depth?

No — you're precisely who this is for. Everything is taught from zero, in order, with a pause after each step so nobody gets left behind. The most common thing people say afterwards is that they wish they'd done it years ago.

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing at all. Every product, brush, mirror and disposable is provided. Optionally, bring your own foundation if you already like it, and any product you've never worked out how to use — we'll solve it together. Come with a clean face and no moisturiser if you can, since skin prep is the first thing we do.

My daughter is 14. Can she come?

Yes. Teens from 13 are very welcome with a signed parent consent form, which includes a medical and allergy disclosure and a photo permission section. You're welcome to stay for the session — several parents do — or to drop off and collect. I hold a current Working With Children Check and full public liability insurance.

Will you be putting makeup on my face?

Only if you'd like light help, and only after asking first — every single time. Everyone applies everything to their own face, at their own mirror. I demonstrate on myself. Nobody applies makeup to anybody else.

I have sensitive skin and allergies.

Please tell me on the booking form and I'll prepare for it. You can patch test anything on your wrist before it goes near your face, and you're always welcome to use your own products for any step instead.

Is there a shade that will match me?

Yes. I carry a full foundation range across warm, cool and neutral undertones. Being properly matched is not negotiable in my room.

Will I look overdone walking out?

No — and this is deliberate. The last thing we do together is remove everything properly and moisturise. You leave with clean, calm skin and a set of notes, not a full evening face at four in the afternoon.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days out, your deposit transfers to any future class. Inside 7 days it isn't refundable, as your seat is held and your products are prepared. If you wake up unwell, just message me — I'll move you to the next date at no cost. Please don't come in sick.
