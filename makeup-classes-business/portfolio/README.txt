Aphrodite's genuine client work — original photographs.

Drop original, unedited photos into this folder.

Before using any identifiable face in marketing, obtain the client's
permission. Past clients predate the consent form in 01-legal-and-safety.md,
so a short message asking permission is required. Where no permission is
given, use non-identifiable crops only (eyes, lips, cheek detail).

Planned processing: colour grading to the brand palette, cropping to
Instagram ratios (1080x1350 feed, 1080x1920 story, 1080x1080 grid),
exposure and white-balance correction.

These photographs take priority over generated imagery for the flyer hero,
portfolio posts and the "My Work" highlight. Generated images are for
flat-lays, backgrounds, story templates and room shots.
