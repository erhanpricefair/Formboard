# Gentle Flow — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Gentle Flow is made for people who'd rather not hand over their personal details just to do a bit of gentle exercise. So we've built it to collect as little as possible.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Gentle Flow app. It's written in plain English on purpose.

## The short version

- You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
- Your plan, your progress and your settings are stored **on your own device**, not on our servers.
- We don't sell your information to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.
- We can't see your practice history. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

## What's stored on your device

When you use Gentle Flow, the app saves the following on your iPhone or iPad:

- Your answers to the setup questions (your goals, movement level, chair preference, any joints you've told us to be gentle with, and your preferred session length)
- The practice plan generated from those answers
- Which sessions you've completed, and when
- Any mood check-ins or notes you choose to add
- Your accessibility settings, such as high contrast and text size
- Any sessions you've downloaded for offline use

This information stays on your device. We have no way of reading it.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. You can also clear it at any time from **Settings → Clear My Data** inside the app.

## Backups

If you have iCloud Backup switched on for your device, your Gentle Flow information may be included in your device backup. That backup belongs to you and is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not ours. We have no access to it.

## Subscriptions and payments

Gentle Flow Premium is sold through the App Store. That means:

- **Apple handles the payment**, not us. We never see your card details, billing address, or any other payment information.
- We receive anonymous sales reports from Apple showing how many subscriptions were purchased. These don't identify individual customers.
- Your subscription is managed in your device Settings, under your Apple Account. You can cancel there at any time.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Sharing with family or carers

Gentle Flow includes an option to share a short summary of your practice with a family member or carer. This only ever happens when you choose it:

- Nothing is sent automatically
- You choose what to send it with — messages, email, or anything else on your device
- The summary goes directly from your device to whoever you send it to; it doesn't pass through us

## Analytics and tracking

Gentle Flow does not include any advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking software. We don't build a profile of you, and we don't follow you across other apps or websites.

## Children

Gentle Flow isn't designed for or directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

## Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to access and correct personal information an organisation holds about you.

Because Gentle Flow keeps your information on your own device and not on our servers, you already have full access to everything the app stores — you can view it in the app, and delete it at any time via **Settings → Clear My Data**.

If you believe we've handled your information improperly, you're welcome to contact us using the details below. You can also make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
