**Please speak with your GP or a physiotherapist before starting**, particularly if you:

- have a heart condition, high blood pressure, or dizziness
- have had a recent fall, injury, surgery, or hospital stay
- have arthritis, osteoporosis, or joint replacements
- take medication that affects your balance or blood pressure

**Stop straight away** if you feel pain, dizziness, breathlessness, or discomfort, and seek medical advice.

Please keep a sturdy chair, rail, or bench within reach during standing sessions, especially when you're starting out. Practise on a level, non-slip surface with room to move, and never practise anywhere you might fall against something hard.
